The Man Behind the Flame

A Chef Who Cooks With Soul

I am Reza Aran — born in Tehran, trained by fire, refined by the world. My culinary journey began in 2006, not in a school, but in the kitchens where real food is born: smoky, imperfect, and unforgettable.

Over 17 years and 3 countries, I have led kitchens from Tehran to Kuala Lumpur — designing menus from scratch, building brigades, and inventing over 100 signature recipes that have defined the dining experiences of multiple establishments.

"I am not just a chef — I am a teller of flavors. Every plate is a story I refuse to repeat."

My brand, Aran — Most Delicious, is built on a simple conviction: that food is the most honest form of creativity. No shortcuts. No compromise. Just the relentless pursuit of the perfect bite.

The Journey

Senior Chef de Cuisine

Personally trained and mentored junior chefs, instilling a culture of precision and creativity. Built menus from scratch that became defining features of each venue.

2012 — 2018

Chef & Creative Lead

🇮🇷 Tehran, Iran — High-End Restaurants & Private Catering

Developed a personal repertoire of 100+ original recipes across multiple cuisines. Established private catering operations for luxury events, corporate functions, and high-profile gatherings. This period crystallized the signature Aran philosophy: flavor-first, technique-driven, story-led.

2006 — 2012

Foundational Training & Rise

🇮🇷 Tehran, Iran — Traditional & Fine Dining Kitchens

Mastered foundational classical techniques across seafood preparations, dough artistry, complex sauce construction, and open-fire cooking. Built the physical and intellectual toolkit that underpins every dish that carries the Aran name today.

2010 — 2011

Executive Chef

Led international kitchen operations with a team of 12+ culinary professionals. Designed and implemented a complete menu overhaul introducing 20+ signature fusion dishes blending Western, Asian, and Mediterranean techniques. Elevated dining standards to luxury hospitality benchmarks.
